# iPhone 18 Concept Live 60s v1 — Bilingual Vertical Preproduction

## Release status and truth boundary

- As of 2026-09-06, Apple has announced a special event for 2026-09-09 but has not officially announced iPhone 18 Pro Max or a foldable iPhone.
- The current official iPhone lineup is iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air, iPhone 17, iPhone 17e and iPhone 16.
- Therefore this case is a disclosed pre-release concept livestream. It must not display a checkout price, inventory count, delivery promise, discount, or an Apple endorsement.
- Calls to action are limited to “set event reminder”, “follow official release”, and “save comparison checklist”.

## Media lineage

- `portrait-zh` and `portrait-en` are independent 1080 × 1920 variants with separate native speech scripts and future model lineages.
- No neural camera plate has been generated yet. The interactive prototype uses an explicit camera placeholder and is not a final video.
- Future model work may generate only the host, concept devices, physical counter, lighting and motion. All readable text, comments, counters, product facts, buttons, captions and gifts remain Code/HyperFrames layers.
- Paid generation requires a separate concrete maximum call count. Never retry automatically and never overwrite a prior result.

## Style prompt

A high-energy vertical tech livestream with a candid phone-demo counter, dark graphite camera stage, luminous glass UI, dense but legible comments, a pinned viewer question, rapid host responses, and restrained commerce motion. It should feel like a real live room while remaining visibly labeled as a synthetic pre-release concept—not an official Apple Store or a fake in-stock sale.

## Layout

- 1080 × 1920 vertical canvas.
- Top: host identity, LIVE state, viewer count, and permanent synthetic/pre-release disclosure.
- Center: uninterrupted camera plate safe zone. Host face occupies upper-middle; two concept devices stay below the face.
- Lower-left: maximum four active comments. The current question is promoted to a pinned response chip.
- Lower-right: like/share/reminder actions.
- Bottom: non-purchase information drawer and input composer.

## Motion

- Comments enter as a deterministic waterfall and nudge upward when the queue advances.
- A question becomes a pinned chip 400–700 ms before the matching spoken answer; `responds_to` connects both tracks.
- Gifts use finite anticipate → burst → settle timelines and never obscure the host face or device hinge/camera area.
- Camera continuity uses matched hand position, lens height, device state and terminal frame contracts; no theatrical wipes.

## What not to do

- No invented official specifications, prices, stock, preorder dates or guaranteed product name.
- No Apple or TikTok logo, official-store identity, cloned public-figure voice, or misleading endorsement.
- No generated readable text inside the neural plate.
- No random animation, infinite loop, silent model substitution, or automatic paid retry.
- Keep `training_eligible=false` until technical QA, independent semantic/visual review and explicit human approval all pass.
